# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Muse is for people composing, editing and listening to music expressed as YAML.
The browser player supports a local edit, render, listen and revise workflow,
without uploading the score or requiring a synthesis backend.

## Product Purpose

Make a text score easy to audition and refine. Keep the score, playback position,
render state and listening controls understandable in one workspace.

## Brand Personality

Expressive, tactile, musical. The user selected a music-studio character.
Use direct, helpful language and purposeful physical control cues.

## Anti-references

No specific reference products or anti-references were supplied. Avoid obscuring
core playback behind decorative studio equipment or ambiguous controls.

## Design Principles

- Keep playback within reach while editing.
- Distinguish the current score from previously rendered audio.
- Show actual audio and progress rather than decorative visualisations.
- Separate listening adjustments from score changes and export.
- Keep advanced rendering explanations available without dominating the workspace.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Preserve labelled native controls, keyboard operation, visible focus and reduced
motion support. No additional individual accessibility requirements were supplied.
