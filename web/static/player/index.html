Help & shortcuts

⌘ / Ctrl + Enter renders the score. Space pauses or resumes when you are outside a control.

Seeking into audio that is still rendering waits for it to arrive. A long note or dense chord can take longer to prepare. If playback pauses to buffer, try the full-score mode.

The browser player supports procedural Muse instruments. Neural singing needs the local CLI. Lyric-based scat prepares its vocal track before progressive playback.
