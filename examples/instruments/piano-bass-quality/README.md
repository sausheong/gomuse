# Piano and upright-bass refinement

study.yaml supplies the identical dry solo notes and dynamics for both renders. before-raw.wav was captured before changing the engine; after-raw.wav uses the refined presets. The study plays piano first, then upright bass, including repeated notes and contrasting dynamics.

before.wav and after.wav match each instrument segment independently to -23 dBFS RMS, with 10 ms edge fades and 400 ms between instruments. Separate piano/bass matched files are also provided. No compression or limiting is applied. RMS matching is approximate rather than perceptual LUFS.

misty4-before.wav preserves the previous engine render. scores/misty5.yaml keeps its arrangement and uses the refined synthesis. Re-rendering Misty 4 now also uses the new presets, so use the preserved audio for comparisons. The pedal resonance is a local resonator approximation, not a full coupled piano. Audio metrics do not establish listening preference.
